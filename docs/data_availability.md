# Data availability

- **Holstein SNP panel** (n=1092, 164,312 autosomal SNPs; milk/fat/SCS traits): restricted;
  available from the corresponding author on reasonable request, subject to the data providers' agreements.
- **Japanese Black (snp3)** (n=9850; additive/dominance relationship matrices; six carcass traits):
  relationship-matrix level only; no individual genotypes are distributed.
- **Not uploaded:** raw genotype/phenotype files, GRM `.npy` matrices, fold-level prediction CSVs
  containing real phenotypes, and any farm-identifying information.
- **Provided:** the exact sanitised analysis scripts, the experiment index, configs, and the
  table/figure regeneration logic. Reviewers/editors may request repository access during review.
- **Mock data:** small synthetic inputs may be placed under `data/processed_placeholder/` to test
  that the pipeline runs; they are clearly synthetic and never reported as results.
